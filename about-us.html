About SureFix

SureFix is a brand of S Doshi Papers Industries Private Limited, representing over four decades of experience in manufacturing reliable, application-specific self-adhesive tape solutions.

Built on ethical business practices, disciplined manufacturing, and a strong commitment to quality, SureFix focuses on creating products that simplify industrial processes, improve reliability, and deliver consistent performance in real-world conditions across multiple industries.

Our Journey

Rooted in Experience. Evolved with Industry Needs.

SureFix traces its origins to S Doshi Papers Industries Private Limited, established in 1984 and headquartered in Mumbai, India. The company began its journey by manufacturing hot melt adhesive–based label stock for offset printers, supporting pharmaceutical and FMCG supply chains through leading printers serving organisations such as Hindustan Lever Limited, Cipla, Glaxo, and other pharmaceutical manufacturers.

With the evolution of office-based printing technologies, the company was among the early and large vendors supplying adhesive solutions for dot matrix printer labels, adapting proactively to changing industry needs.

In the 1990s, the company expanded into bag closure tapes, including application-specific solutions developed for Reliance Industries Limited (RIL). Over time, this continuous focus on solving real application challenges led to the growth of SureFix as a dedicated brand, offering a wide range of self-adhesive tape solutions for industrial and commercial applications.

Today, SureFix operates from South Goa and Andheri East, Mumbai, serving customers across industries with consistency and commitment.

How We Do It

Manufacturing Built on Precision & Control

Our manufacturing process is structured around proven technology, process discipline, and quality assurance:

  • European slot-die coating line for uniform adhesive coating
  • Complete in-house operations including coating, rewinding, slitting, slicing, testing, and packing
  • Fundamental process controls to ensure repeatability
  • Dedicated production and quality assurance teams
  • Advanced testing infrastructure including Universal Testing Machine and Shear Adhesion Failure Tester & an online GSM checking machine
