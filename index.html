About Us

Four Decades of Adhesive Manufacturing Experience

Established in 1984, S Doshi Papers Industries Private Limited is an Indian manufacturer of self-adhesive tape solutions, operating under the brand name SureFix. The company began its journey by manufacturing hot melt adhesive-based label stock for offset printers serving pharmaceutical and FMCG industries, supporting supply chains of leading organisations during the early years of growth.

Over the decades, SureFix has evolved into a multi-product adhesive solutions company, operating from South Goa and Mumbai. With complete in-house manufacturing capabilities and a disciplined process-driven approach, we focus on developing application-specific adhesive solutions that deliver consistent performance under real-world conditions. Our growth has been organic, ethical, and built on long-term customer relationships.
